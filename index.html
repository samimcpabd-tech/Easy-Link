Why Easy Link

What keeps IT managers up at night

Bangladeshi enterprises face a distinct set of challenges: vendors who disappear after installation, fragmented solutions, and zero local support when something fails at 11 PM. Easy Link was built around these exact pain points.

  • ⚡
    Downtime kills productivity Network outages cost enterprises BDT 2–5 lakh per hour. Most BD vendors lack 24/7 SLA-backed support.
  • 🔐
    Cybersecurity gaps are widening 80%+ of Dhaka SMEs have no UTM firewall. Ransomware attacks in BD grew 3× since 2022.
  • 🧩
    Too many vendors, no single accountability Hardware from one, software from another — nobody owns the whole picture.
  • 📈
    Digital transformation stalls without guidance IT teams lack a roadmap partner who understands the Bangladeshi enterprise context.
